     * Companies use automated software called ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems).

### 🔹 Slide 3: What is an ATS & Resume Score in Simple Terms?
* **Category**: `SIMPLE EXPLANATION`
* **Header**: **Understanding 'ATS' & 'Resume Scores' Without Any Jargon**
* **Subtitle**: Think of it like a smart grocery checklist or a master chef's recipe.
* **Card 1: 🍰 The Master Chef Recipe Analogy**:
  * **The Job Description** = A Master Chef's Recipe (e.g. Needs flour, cocoa, eggs, 5 years baking experience).
  * **Your Resume** = Your Basket of Ingredients (The skills, projects, and work history you bring).
  * **The ATS (Applicant Tracking System)** = The kitchen assistant checking if the basket matches the recipe.
  * **The Match Score (0 - 100%)** = A fair percentage showing how well your experience covers the required recipe.
* **Card 2: Old Robot Way vs. Resume Screening Agent**:
  * ❌ **Old Dumb Robots**: If the job says "Customer Support" and you wrote "Client Assistance", it rejects you because the exact spelling didn't match!
  * ✅ **Resume Screening Agent**: Understands human meaning! It knows "Client Assistance" means you can help customers, checks real experience, and gives honest feedback.
* **🗣️ Speaker Script**:
  > *"If you've ever wondered what 'ATS score' means, think of a cake recipe. If the recipe calls for chocolate and eggs, and your basket has vanilla and butter, you're a different match. But old systems were dumb: if you wrote 'Cacao' instead of 'Cocoa', they threw out your basket! Resume Screening Agent understands the real meaning behind your words."*

### 🔹 Slide 4: Meet Resume Screening Agent - The Solution
* **Category**: `THE SOLUTION`
* **Header**: **Meet Resume Screening Agent: Your 4-Agent Hiring Committee**
* **Subtitle**: Instead of relying on one single AI guess, 4 specialized AI Agents work together.
* **4 Agent Team Cards**:
  1. **🔍 Agent 1: The Reader (Resume Parser)**
     * Reads PDF or Word files without making formatting errors.
     * Organizes skills, education, and years of experience neatly.
  2. **⚖️ Agent 2: The Grader (Review Agent)**
     * Scores candidates fairly from 0 to 100 on 4 pillars: Technical Skills, Experience, Education, and Role Fit.
  3. **🛡️ Agent 3: The Auditor (Quality Assurance)**
     * The Fact-Checker. Prevents fake scores, verifies mathematical overlap, and ensures 100% honesty.
  4. **👑 Agent 4: The Coach (Ranker & Interviewer)**
     * Ranks the best candidates and writes personalized interview questions tailored to each candidate's gaps.
* **🗣️ Speaker Script**:
  > *"When a top company hires, they don't rely on just one person. They have an intake coordinator, a technical interviewer, a quality auditor, and a hiring manager. Resume Screening Agent brings this exact 4-person expert team into an automated AI workflow."*

### 🔹 Slide 5: How It Works: Step-by-Step Flow
* **Category**: `WORKFLOW`
* **Header**: **How It Works: Step-by-Step in Plain English**
* **Subtitle**: From uploading a resume to getting a complete hiring report in under 30 seconds.
* **4 Flow Steps**:
  * **Step 1: Upload 📥** — Drop in 10, 50, or 100 resumes (PDF, Word, TXT) and paste the job requirements.
  * **Step 2: Math Check 📊** — The system calculates mathematical topic similarity to establish an honest baseline.
  * **Step 3: Multi-Agent Audit 🤖** — The 4 AI agents read, score, fact-check, and audit every claim.
  * **Step 4: Actionable Report 🎯** — Get a ranked leaderboard, clear skill gap breakdowns, and custom interview questions!
